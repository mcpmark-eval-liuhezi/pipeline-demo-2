# Release Checklist

## Before you merge

1. **Build** — build artifacts are produced from the exact commit under review.
2. **Test** — all automated tests pass, including the `test` job in the CI Checklist workflow.
3. **Review** — at least one reviewer has gone through the diff.
4. **Merge** — squash-merge once the above are satisfied.

## Notes

- Keep this checklist short; it is the lightweight pilot before any team-wide rollout.
- Update it when the workflow changes.
